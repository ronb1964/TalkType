# Real samples of Ron writing

## Reading these

Everything here was typed or dictated by Ron with no AI involved. That is the
whole point of the file, so guard it. If something was drafted or polished by a
model, it does not belong here no matter how much it sounds like him.

The set is small. Five short messages from one session on 2026-09-15, plus
whatever has been added since. Small is still better than large-and-contaminated:
a handful of genuine lines teaches the rhythm, while a pile of AI-assisted prose
teaches the opposite of what we want.

## What is NOT a sample

Do not treat these as voice references, even though they are published under his
name:

- `CHANGELOG.md`, `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md` in the TalkType repo
- Existing GitHub issue and discussion comments
- Commit messages (the entire history carries `Co-Authored-By: Claude`)

All of it was AI-drafted. Learning from it is how you end up producing the thing
Ron keeps rejecting.

## What these show

- **Short.** Three of the five are a single sentence. One is three words.
- **Clauses chained with "or" and "and"** where writing would break the sentence:
  "I need you to try to change it or if there's a skill or a plug-in or something
  that will help with writing things like this so it doesn't sound like AI."
- **Trailing vagueness** rather than false precision: "or something".
- **No preamble.** He opens with the request. No "hope you're well", no framing.
- **The verdict stated flat**, no cushioning: "Still doesn't really sound like
  me. I could tell it's AI written."
- **"really" as his hedge** of choice, where written prose would pick
  "particularly" or "entirely".
- **Zero formatting.** No bullets, no bold, no headers, no em dashes.
- **Dictation artefacts** ("id like", "Ai", "github" lowercase) which are noise
  to strip, not voice to copy.

## Adding to this file

When Ron writes something himself, drop it in with the date and who it was for.
Old emails, forum posts, texts, comments on other people's repos, anything at
all as long as no model touched it. Longer pieces are especially valuable, since
the current set shows how he opens and how blunt he is but says almost nothing
about how he sustains an explanation over several paragraphs.
